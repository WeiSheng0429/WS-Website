Hi! I'm Wei Sheng.

Hi there, as you already saw I am Leon, a UX Designer with a unique background. Before I became a UX Designer, I worked as a tennis teacher and ski instructor for five years. Those experiences helped me develop strong communication and problem-solving skills that have been invaluable in my design career.

While finishing my degree in Economics, I discovered a passion for UX Design and decided to pursue it as a career. To deepen my knowledge and skills, I enrolled in a college for UX Design. Since then, I've been fully immersed in the industry, continuously learning and growing as a professional.

In my free time, you'll probably find me behind a camera, capturing moments and scenes through photography. It's a hobby that allows me to express creativity in a different medium and complements my work as a designer.
